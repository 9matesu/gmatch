## VERIFIED CLEAN (no change)

- security-greps battery: ALL GREPS CLEAN.
- Stream endpoint capability model holds: unguessable id + server-bound
  upstream, SSRF guarded at create time (`isPrivateTarget`, protocol+length
  validation in `resolveMedia`), Range passthrough, 500MB cap.
- Chat rate limit (2s), member cap 8, name/queue/chat clamps: correct.
- A7 hypothesis REJECTED: legal corpus does NOT contradict the vault
  promise. documents.ts states local encrypted history explicitly ("key
  holder's hands"); image caches are RAM-only (cachePolicy.ts F1 note). No
  dead-code hit either: `vaultPrune` is wired through preload +
  gmatch-bridge.
- Watch store sync math, clock offset, drift correction: unchanged from
  rodada 4, still test-covered.
- Bulk video route: sequential upstream fetch, count-clamped (10/request),
  budget-gated. No open-proxy widening (upstream bound at create).

## Watch-out (accepted, documented)

- `takeParseBudget` is per-isolate in-memory (fine at 50-room scale;
  multi-isolate needs Durable Object counters).
- The global swarm still relays non-JSON/opaque frames raw (legacy P2P
  wire); any NEW client->client typed frame must get an explicit handler
  with auth, never the fallback path.
- Chrome autoplay: tabs that never received a click park at 0:00 on
  overnight rooms; manual resync chip is the recovery, by design.
